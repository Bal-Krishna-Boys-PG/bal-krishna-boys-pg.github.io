Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about living, dining, rules, and booking at Balkrishna Boys' PG.

We offer four distinct room sharing options to match student and professional budgets:

  • 2 Sharing: ₹10,000/month (Non-AC, Spacious layout with General Washroom)
  • 3 Sharing: ₹8,000/month (Non-AC) or ₹9,000/month (AC with Attached Washroom + AC electricity usage)
  • 4 Sharing: ₹8,000/month (Non-AC) or ₹9,000/month (AC with Attached Washroom + AC electricity usage)
  • 5 Sharing: ₹8,000/month (Non-AC, 1 Exclusive group room on property)

All pricing includes daily 3 times Pure Veg meals, high-speed Wi-Fi, and daily housekeeping.

Yes, 100% Pure Vegetarian breakfast, lunch, and dinner are fully included in the monthly rent with zero extra food charges. Meals are prepared fresh in our hygienic in-house kitchen with rotating menus of wholesome Gujarati and North Indian homestyle dishes. We also prepare 100% pure Jain food upon request without onion, garlic, or root vegetables.

No curfew whatsoever. We provide 24/7 open gate access. Whether you have late-night university library hours, exam prep sessions, or rotating office shifts, you have complete entry and exit freedom at any hour without hassle.

Regular everyday electricity—including ceiling fans, tube lights, laptop/phone chargers, and common area lighting—is completely covered in your monthly rent. For AC rooms (₹9,000/mo in 3 and 4 sharing), individual digital sub-meters track exact AC unit consumption, which is divided equally among room occupants based on actual government tariff rates.

We collect a 100% refundable security deposit equal to 1 month's rent at admission. When you plan to vacate, we ask for a standard 30-day prior notice so our team can process your refund and room clearance smoothly.

Each boy receives an individual box bed with comfortable mattress, pillow, bedsheet, an individual lockable steel wardrobe for valuables, a study desk, and a study chair. Additionally, the PG provides high-speed Wi-Fi, fully automatic washing machines for clothes, daily housekeeping, RO drinking water, and winter water geysers.

Parents and family are always welcome during daytime visiting hours (9:00 AM – 9:00 PM). For male parents traveling from other cities, temporary overnight accommodations can be arranged with prior intimation to PG management. Female family members may meet residents comfortably in our common reception area.

You can schedule an in-person PG visit by calling owner Kashyap Patel directly at +91 63535 78799 or sending an instant message via WhatsApp (opens in new tab). If you are located outside Ahmedabad, our manager can also provide live video tours of available rooms.
