Privacy Policy

Transparent data collection, handling, and deletion practices for inquiries submitted to Bal Krishna Boys' PG Ahmedabad.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Bal Krishna Boys' PG ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide paying guest hostel accommodations for male students and working professionals at 35, Nitin Nagar Society, Near Sola Railway Crossing, Science City Road, Ghatlodiya, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380061.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, and process personal information when you visit our website (https://bal-krishna-boys-pg.github.io) or submit inquiries regarding room bookings and visiting appointments.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when filling out our website inquiry form or reaching out via WhatsApp or phone call:

  • Full Name: To address you and register your inquiry.
  • Contact Phone Number: To contact you via call or WhatsApp with pricing, availability, and scheduling details.
  • Email Address: Optional, used to send detailed accommodation confirmations.
  • Occupation Status: Student, working professional, or parent.
  • Accommodation Preferences: Sharing preference (2, 3, 4, or 5 sharing), room cooling type (AC or Non-AC), expected move-in date, and dietary preferences (such as Jain food requests).

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Responding to room availability and pricing inquiries.
  • Scheduling in-person PG facility visits during visiting hours (9:00 AM – 9:00 PM).
  • Verifying admission documentation upon physical check-in (in accordance with local housing requirements).
  • We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal contact information to any third-party marketing companies.

4. Third-Party Services & Technical Integrations

To operate our website securely and reliably on GitHub Pages, we integrate the following verified third-party services:

  • Formspree: Website contact form inquiries are transmitted over HTTPS to Formspree, which relays your request directly to our official inbox (balkrishnaboyspg@gmail.com). Formspree processes form data under rigorous industry data protection standards.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect our web forms against automated spam and bot abuse. Turnstile evaluates non-sensitive browser signals without using invasive tracking cookies or biometric puzzles.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): We utilize Google Analytics to understand overall website traffic patterns (e.g., page views, approximate geographic region, browser types). All IP addresses are anonymized, and no personally identifiable information (PII) is shared with Google Analytics.
  • Elfsight: Used to display verified public reviews from our Google Business Profile.

5. Cookies and Telemetry

Our website uses minimal, standard cookies strictly necessary for Google Analytics traffic telemetry and Cloudflare spam mitigation. You can configure your browser to reject cookies or alert you when cookies are sent; doing so will not impair your browsing experience on our website.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate SSL/TLS encryption across our entire domain to ensure that your data transmitted between your web browser and our website remains secure and private.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, or to request that we correct or delete your inquiry details from our email records. To exercise these rights, simply email us at balkrishnaboyspg@gmail.com.
