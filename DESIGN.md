# Design System: Bal Krishna Boys' PG Ahmedabad

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Frosted Daylight Prospectus"**

Bal Krishna Boys' PG Ahmedabad is organized as an open, daylight-lit architectural prospectus built for equal verification by prospective college residents and their visiting parents. Rather than opaque dark cards or warm wood-and-parchment tones, the interface sets translucent frosted acrylic panes across an ambient sky-and-sunlight canvas streaked with soft Deep Crest Navy and golden Marigold radial light blooms. Every tariff, meal schedule, sub-metering clause, and open-gate policy reads effortlessly inside crisp sapphire-bordered folios and unified hairline ledgers.

This material hierarchy bridges institutional parental trust and young-adult autonomy in a single glance: Deep Crest Navy (`#032B52`) anchors headings, navigation rails, smoked contrast bands, and primary booking triggers, while calibrated Marigold Gold (`#874D00`–`#F8C156`) signals verified property details, active tier selections, and hospitable warmth. The system explicitly rejects legacy brown-and-sandstone hostel palettes, dark-mode hero slabs, synthetic stock photography, fabricated testimonials, stacked eyebrow pills above headlines, and heavy side-stripe accent borders (`4px` left/right bars).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Translucent frosted acrylic containers (`77%`–`90%` white fill with `14px`–`24px` backdrop blur and saturation boost) floating over continuous sky-to-sunlight radial blooms.
- Authority-driven Crest Navy (`#032B52`) paired with WCAG-stratified Marigold accents (`#D5890A` active borders, `#F8C156` dark-surface highlights, `#874D00`/`#6A3B00` light-pane ink).
- Geometric `Sora` display headlines anchored cleanly above tabular `Figtree` pricing columns (`tabular-nums`) and `1px` architectural hairlines (`rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.10–0.14)`).
- Full hardware and accessibility resilience via `@supports not (backdrop-filter)`, `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)`, and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` fallbacks.

### Named Rules
**The Marigold Contrast Rule.** Bright sunlit marigold shades (`#F8C156`, `#D5890A`) may only act as structural bottom borders, dark-surface text/icons (`>= 4.5:1` on Crest Navy `#032B52`), or dark-ink button fills (`#021A33` on `#F8C156`); light-pane text, inline highlights, and status badges must step down to Marigold Bronze (`#874D00` for large display accents `>= 3:1` and UI icons) or Marigold Deep (`#6A3B00` for readable badges and copy `>= 4.5:1`).

**The Single-Semantic Validation Rule.** Inline validation errors and alert banners reserve crimson (`#B91C1C` border/copy, `#991B1B` on `#FEF2F2` banner) strictly for invalid inputs and submission failures; all brand authority remains Crest Navy (`#032B52`) paired with Marigold accents, avoiding off-palette greens or purples.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Sora (weights `600`, `700`, `800`, fallback `sans-serif`)
**Body Font:** Figtree (weights `400`, `500`, `600`, `700`, `800`, fallback `sans-serif`)

**Character:** Geometric, open-aperture display numerals and headlines (`Sora`) establish crisp architectural confidence above neutral, high-x-height tabular reading prose (`Figtree`), optimizing rapid scanning of room rates, meal rules, and phone numbers on mobile screens.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (`800` Extrabold, `1.875rem` mobile -> `3rem` `sm` -> `3.35rem` `xl`, `1.08` line-height, `-0.025em` tracking): Primary hero statement (`<h1>`) and architectural numerical callout (`404` status plaque).
- **Headline** (`800` Extrabold, `1.875rem` mobile -> `2.25rem` `sm+`, `1.2` line-height, `-0.025em` tracking): Major section titles (`<h2>`) across Rooms, Services, Rules, Reviews, FAQ, Inquiry, and `thank-you` confirmation headings.
- **Title** (`700` Bold, `1.125rem`–`1.25rem`, `1.35` line-height, `-0.015em` tracking): Room card titles (`2 Sharing Room` through `5 Sharing Room` enforced single-line via `whitespace-nowrap`), feature block titles, header wordmark (`Bal Krishna Boys' PG`), and policy clauses.
- **Body** (`400`–`500`, `0.875rem`–`1.0625rem` hero lead `15px`/`17px`, `1.625` line-height): Narrative paragraphs constrained to `54ch`–`70ch` max-width with `[text-wrap:pretty]` on hero leads.
- **Label** (`700`–`800`, `0.625rem`–`0.75rem`, `1.25` line-height, `0.025em`–`0.05em` tracking): Form labels (uppercase), buttons, navigation links (`0.875rem` semibold), table metadata, and inline status badges (`10px`–`11px`).

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Tariff Rule.** Apply geometric display `Sora` exclusively to headings, wordmarks, and price figures, while locking every rent tariff (`₹8,000`–`₹10,000`), phone number (`+91 63535 78799`), visiting hour, carousel index, and input numeral to `Figtree` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so numbers align cleanly across interactive state switches.

**The Head-First Hierarchy Rule.** Headlines (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>`) open cleanly on frosted surfaces with `text-wrap: balance`, never preceded by stacked uppercase pill kickers above the heading line.

## Layout

All routes anchor inside a centered page container (`1280px` max-width on `index.html`, headers, and footers; `1024px` on `404.html`; `896px` on `privacy-policy.html` and FAQ accordions; `768px` on `thank-you.html`) with responsive horizontal padding (`16px` mobile -> `24px` `640px+` -> `32px` `1024px+`). Sections maintain an intentional `64px` mobile / `80px` desktop vertical rhythm (`py-16 sm:py-20`) separated by `1px` perimeter hairlines (`rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.10)`).

At the top of every route floats a single-row Sticky Acrylic Navigation Rail (`h-[4.5rem]` / `72px`, `rounded-2xl`), automatically condensing the sub-brand location line on narrow viewports (`Science City, Ahmedabad` `< 640px`; `Sola & Science City Road • Ahmedabad` `>= 640px`) to prevent multi-line overflow at `390px` width. Below it on `index.html`, the first viewport uses an asymmetric `12-column` split (`7 cols` frosted Hero Prospectus Folio + Interactive Room-Spec Switcher on the left paired with a `5 cols` Translucent Photo Bezel carousel on the right). Subsequent bands alternate between a `4-column` room pricing grid (`md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4`), a unified 3-row hairline-divided Services Matrix, a `5/7` House Rules split with a `2x2` divided charter grid, an embedded Google Reviews strip, 3 grouped FAQ category ledgers, and a `7/5` dual-action Inquiry Form and Google Map stack. On mobile viewports (`< 768px`), `#mobile-sticky-bar` remains retracted until `window.scrollY > 260`, then slides cleanly into view with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` clearance.

### Named Rules
**The Single-Boundary Grouping Rule.** Group related daily utilities and charter rules inside one outer rounded translucent container divided by hairline rules (`1px` at `rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.10–0.14)`) rather than nesting individual bordered boxes inside an outer card.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is expressed through a hybrid optical model combining real translucent backdrop refraction (`.acrylic-*` panes filtering scrolled ambient navy/marigold radial blooms), crisp top-edge specular inset highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)`), low-opacity structural borders, and soft ambient drop shadows calibrated strictly to surface priority. At rest, surfaces feel crisp and buoyant; `.acrylic-pane-interactive` cards deepen their perimeter shadow and transition border strokes smoothly from cool navy (`rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.14)`) to warm marigold bronze (`rgba(135, 77, 0, 0.42)`) on `:hover` or `:focus-within` (`0.25s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Every translucent layer declares solid `@supports not (backdrop-filter)` and `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)` fallbacks (`#FFFFFF`, `#EEF3F9`, `#032B52`) so low-transparency preferences preserve full contrast without blur.

### Named Rules
**The Frosted Refraction Rule.** Frosted panes achieve physical depth through real backdrop refraction (`blur(14px–24px) saturate(150%–175%)`), inner white top-edge specular highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)`), and calibrated sapphire perimeter hairlines over slow-drifting daylight radial washes—never through opaque flat gray slabs or heavy hard-edged drop shadows, and always paired with `@supports not` and `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` solid fallbacks.

## Shapes

The radius progression maps directly to structural containment scale: crisp rounded controls and inputs (`12px` / `rounded-xl`) nest neatly inside `16px` (`rounded-2xl`) cards, dropdowns, and navigation rails; multi-section ledgers, matrices, and smoked bands step up to `24px` (`rounded-3xl`); flagship hero folios and optical bezels crown the hierarchy at `26px` (`rounded-[26px]`). Status pills, scrollbar thumbs, and active gallery indicator pills (`w-5 h-2`) use full capsule geometry (`9999px`). Structural divisions rely on thin `1px` top/bottom/column hairlines (`rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.10–0.14)`), while active states stamp the bottom edge (`2px`–`3px solid #D5890A`) rather than wrapping vertical left/right accent stripes.

### Named Rules
**The Perimeter Stamp Rule.** Prefer continuous outer perimeter contours (`border` hairline strokes and `2px`–`3px` bottom marigold active border stamps) over heavy vertical accent stripes (`border-l-4` or `border-r-4`), preserving clean card edges and pill-rounded (`9999px`) inline status tags.

## Components

Every interactive primitive maintains a minimum `44px` hit target (`min-h-[44px]`), clear visible focus rings (`2px solid #032B52` on daylight surfaces; `2px solid #F8C156` on smoked navy bands and footers), and crisp state contrast across hover, focus, active tab, and form validation errors.

### Buttons
**Tactile authority with unmistakable bottom marigold stamps on primary conversions.**
- **Shape:** `12px` radius (`rounded-xl`); `8px` (`rounded-lg`) reserved only for compact post-submit WhatsApp/Call fallback pills.
- **Primary Solid Navy:** Crest Navy (`#032B52`) fill, Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text, `2px` bottom Marigold Stamp (`#D5890A`), horizontal padding `16px`–`24px`, vertical `10px`–`16px`. Used for one flagship conversion trigger per viewport context (Header WhatsApp, `#hero-spec-cta`, 3-Sharing AC primary option, Inquiry WhatsApp Submit).
- **Hover / Focus:** Background shifts to `#083766` (`brand-800`); focus draws `2px solid #032B52` outline with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary Outlined / Frosted Ghost / Smoked-Surface CTA:** Secondary outlined uses `2px solid #032B52` on `rgba(255,255,255,0.90)` inverting to solid `#032B52`/`#FFFFFF` on hover (`Schedule a Visit on WhatsApp`, `Return to Homepage`); Tertiary frosted ghost buttons use `rgba(255,255,255,0.75–0.85)` with `1px` sapphire borders (`rgba(3,43,82,0.20–0.25)`); Smoked-surface CTAs invert to `#F8C156` fill with `#021A33` ink (`hover: #FCEFD4`).

### Interactive Room-Spec Switcher (`#hero-spec-dock`)
**Signature bidirectional lens connecting instant tariff comparison with property photos.**
- **Style:** Separated inside the hero folio by top/bottom `1px` hairlines (`border-y border-brand-900/12`), pairing a 4-column sharing selector grid with a live `Sora` price readout and synced `Book [Tier]` button.
- **State:** Active tab locks `#032B52` fill, `#FFFFFF` ink, and a `3px` bottom Marigold Stamp border (`#D5890A`); inactive tabs rest on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)` with `#16314F` text and hover to `#FFFFFF`. Bidirectional sync keeps ARIA tabs, slide opacity (`700ms ease-out`), chip badge (`Verified Property Photos` vs `Live Video Tour Available` on Slide 4), and caption bar locked together via click or `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End` keys.

### Chips & Status Badges
**Compact high-contrast credentials anchored cleanly beside headings or over media corners.**
- **Style:** Filled status tags (`Best Value`, `Only 1 Room`, `Verified Business Profile`) use Marigold Tint (`#FCEFD4`) with Marigold Deep ink (`#6A3B00`) and `9999px` pill radius; the featured `"Most Popular"` badge uses Crest Navy (`#032B52`), Marigold Highlight (`#F8C156`) uppercase label, and `40%` marigold border. Floating `.acrylic-chip` photo labels use `12px` radius over frosted `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`.

### Cards & Ledgers
**Layered frosted panes framed cleanly to organize pricing vs. grouped specifications.**
- **Corner Style:** `16px` (`rounded-2xl`) on individual room cards and Move-In Terms ledger; `24px` (`rounded-3xl`) on unified Amenities and House Rules matrices; `26px` (`rounded-[26px]`) on hero folios and optical media bezels.
- **Background & Border:** Translucent `.acrylic-pane` (`77%` white + `24px` blur) bounded by `1px solid rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.14)`. ### Inputs / Fields
**Crisp sunlit white surfaces engineered for clear form legibility and rule-aware locking.**
- **Style:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)` translucent white fill, `1px solid #5C738D` border, `12px` radius (`rounded-xl`), `12px 16px` padding (`px-4 py-3`), `#081E38` text and `#364F6B` placeholder.

Incompatible selections (`2` or `5 Sharing` locking Cooling Type to Non-AC) switch the select background to `#E2EBF4` (`slate-100`) with `#364F6B` text, `cursor-not-allowed`, and reveal a plain `#0F4375` quick-switch trigger.
- **Error:** Triggers `border-color: #B91C1C`, `2px` ring at `20%` crimson alpha (`ring-red-700/20`), `#B91C1C` inline helper error (`aria-invalid="true"`), and a persistent `#FEF2F2` banner (`#991B1B` ink).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** pair `Sora` display numerals and headings with `Figtree` tabular numerals (`tabular-nums`) on every rent rate, phone number, visiting hour, and gallery indicator.
- **Do** isolate grouped service lists, move-in policies, and rules inside a single frosted `.acrylic-pane` divided by `1px` sapphire hairlines (`rgba(3, 43, 82, 0.10–0.14)`).
- **Do** step marigold ink down to `#874D00` (display accents/icons) or `#6A3B00` (badges/metadata) on translucent white panes, keeping `#F8C156` strictly for smoked navy surfaces (`#032B52`–`#021A33`).
- **Do** keep explicit `@supports not (backdrop-filter)` and `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)` solid surface fallbacks alongside every translucent acrylic class.
- **Do** ensure every interactive control meets a minimum `44px` tap target and preserve existing third-party scripts, verified room photography, and factual tariffs.

### Don't:
- **Don't** stack uppercase eyebrow pill labels above page or card headings (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>`); open headlines directly on the frosted surface.
- **Don't** nest individual shadowed or bordered boxes inside another bordered card when hairline dividers suffice.
- **Don't** apply heavy `4px` left/right vertical border stripes (`border-l-4`, `border-r-4`), neon drop glows, or idle continuous pulse animations (`animate-pulse`) on cards or pills.
- **Don't** use `background-attachment: fixed` parallax layers, opaque neutral grey cards (`bg-stone-*` / `bg-slate-200`), legacy warm browns (`#835028`), or uncalibrated yellow text on white glass.
- **Don't** substitute stock photos or fabricated resident testimonials for unphotographed rooms (represent the 5-sharing unit honestly with its architectural hall layout plate and live video-tour offer).
